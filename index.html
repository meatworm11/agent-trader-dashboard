Business

Arm licenses semiconductor IP — CPU instruction-set architectures and physical microprocessor designs — to chip designers who pay (a) upfront license fees for access to the technology and (b) royalties on every chip shipped. ARM v9 commands materially higher royalty rates than v8, which means ASP tailwinds persist even at flat unit volumes. Revenue is split roughly 53/47 royalties/licenses in FY2026. The durability argument is that ARM architecture is deeply embedded in the silicon supply chain — switching cost is a decade of software ecosystem lock-in, not a quarter's re-training.
